# Reachard logo: reference study and revised brief

Date: 2026-09-05

## Current user direction

The user wants a simple, abstract, memorable identity with the quality of Butter, Pendo, Toast, Target, Slack, Anthropic and Apollo. They rejected the recent blue/coral and blue/purple ribbon concepts as dated in both form and color. Earlier, they liked concept 45, but rejected its rigid variations. Preserve the interest in a lively abstract mark; do not treat two loops, an infinity sign, gradients, or blue/orange as approved requirements. The user wants actual color exploration, not only black mark sheets. The earlier rejection of R monograms still applies.

- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com . The current header uses a dark uppercase wordmark on a warm neutral field. ## Design judgments drawn from the comparison

These are our interpretations, not statements of official design intent unless explicitly attributed above.

1. A memorable overall silhouette takes priority over illustrating multiple product features.
2. Simplicity includes carefully chosen proportions, optical balance, counter shapes, terminal treatment and spacing. Few elements alone do not ensure quality.
3. Shape, wordmark, palette and presentation should read as one identity. A stock wordmark beside a decorative symbol is not a finished logo.
4. Single bright colors can be confident. Multiple colors require a structural reason, as in Slack's repeating units. Muted color is not automatically more sophisticated.
5. Meaning can be indirect: the product promise should guide the character, while the website copy explains the actual workflow.

## Revised Reachard brief

Product promise: give a job seeker a more thoughtful way to reach the right person behind an opportunity.

Desired character: clear, assured, approachable, distinctive; a small amount of surprise in a controlled shape.

Retire from the next exploration: paired winding ribbons, arbitrary thick/thin blob curves, blue/coral split coloring, cyan-to-purple gradients, decorative dots explained as people, forced entryway/letter symbolism, repeated parameter sweeps.

Explore a compact abstract silhouette with one memorable structural decision; design the lowercase reachard wordmark's weight, spacing and terminals alongside it. Study flat signature colors independently, including their relationship to the existing white/gray website and #007aff action buttons. Do not infer that existing button blue dictates the only possible logo color.

Present each future direction in its actual intended color, with a small browser-toolbar mark and a complete wordmark. Keep monochrome as a secondary reproduction check. Compare 3 genuinely different concepts before refining proportions; do not offer another sheet of near-identical loops.

No active web source or public selection site was changed by this research.
